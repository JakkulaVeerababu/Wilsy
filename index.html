A CLEARER PICTURE

Understand the numbers.

Good career decisions start with knowing what a salary figure actually means.

One company. One example role.

Each card shows salary published for one full-time role, converted to Indian Rupees (INR) per year. Engineering is preferred; other roles are labelled. When multiple engineering ranges are available, we select the middle range by midpoint. These are snapshots of advertised pay, not average company packages or guaranteed offers. Openings can close.

Pay tiers, explained

We group companies by the midpoint of that example salary. These are WILSY browsing bands, not employer rankings. Roles, seniority, and countries differ, so a tier is a starting point for exploration.

Salary ≠ total compensation

“Base salary” appears only when the source identifies it as base pay. Other ranges are labelled “Advertised salary”; they may have role-specific terms. Equity, bonus, commission, and benefits are not added to the range. Disclosed equity is shown separately, without estimating its dollar value.

What’s in the collection?

1,000 global technology employers: established names and active or public YC companies with published pay (converted to INR). The YC selection prioritises its top-company designation and reported team size. This is not a ranking of the world’s 1,000 largest companies. Coverage is concentrated in the United States and the YC startup ecosystem.

Follow the source

Established employers link to their careers websites. Startup links open employer-maintained recruiting pages on Y Combinator, labelled “Company jobs on YC.” Every salary example links to its source. Company details and logos come from employer profiles, public brand assets, and the linked data mirror.
