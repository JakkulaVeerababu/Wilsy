OUR MISSION

About Wilsy

A clearer view of your next move.

Wilsy was founded with a simple goal: to make discovering great technology companies easier for students and professionals worldwide.

Navigating the tech job market can be overwhelming. There are countless job boards, middle-men recruiters, and hidden salary expectations. We wanted to build a single place where you can explore top-tier companies, understand their compensation tiers, and go straight to the source—their official careers page.

What We Do

We aggregate and organize data on 1,000+ tech employers, ranging from ambitious early-stage startups to established Fortune 500 giants. By organizing these companies into intuitive salary tiers, we give you a realistic snapshot of what a career there might look like financially.

Our Data

The data on Wilsy is sourced from public job postings, verified company profiles, and public API directories (such as Y Combinator's company directory). We strive to keep links and data as accurate as possible, giving you the best starting point for your career search.
